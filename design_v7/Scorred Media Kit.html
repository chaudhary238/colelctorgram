The Mark in Use

The circle-tick isn't just a logo — it's a system. It appears as the app icon, the "Scorred Verified" badge, empty-state watermarks, and success confirmations. One shape, everywhere.

Voice & Tone

Confident, not loud
We're the trusted authority for collectors. We state facts plainly. "Scorred Verified" — not "100% Guaranteed Authentic!!!"
For collectors, by collectors
We speak the language — grails, in-hand, sealed, PO. Insider, warm, never corporate.
