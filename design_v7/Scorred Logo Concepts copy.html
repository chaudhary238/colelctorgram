Brand Identity

Scorred — Logo Concepts

6 directions for the collector app. Each shown on light + dark.

Concepts
Option A
Score Line
Wordmark with a bold red underline — a literal "score mark". Clean, editorial, scalable.
Option B
Hard Stamp
Bold red stamp block with ink hard-shadow offset. Same DNA as the CollectorHub seal. High-energy.
Option C
Seal + Wordmark
Rotated S seal (same as CollectorHub's aesthetic) + wordmark + tagline. Works at any size as seal icon alone.
Option D
RR Accent
The double-R — the brand's quirk — punched out in red. Makes the distinctive spelling a feature. Very typographic.
Option E
Price Tag
Collector's price tag as the icon mark. Nods to buying/selling/scoring items. Instantly reads as marketplace.
Option F
Checkmark
A red tick floating above the "rr" — scored = verified/got it. Nods to both the collector "score" and ownership verification.

Designer's note

C (Seal + Wordmark) and D (RR Accent) carry the most long-term brand equity — C because it's versatile (seal alone = app icon), D because it turns the quirky double-R spelling into the identity itself. B (Hard Stamp) is the most energetic and fits the collector-culture aesthetic. E (Price Tag) is the most literal and marketplace-forward.

Selected Direction — Refined
Option F — Tick & Case Exploration

5 tick styles × lowercase vs title case · recommendation marked ✦

Tick style exploration · "Scorred" title case · on light
T1 · Thin
Elegant, subtle. Can disappear at small sizes.
T2 · Medium
Balanced. Reads well at most sizes.
T3 · Bold ✦
Recommended. Matches the weight of Geist 900. Assertive, distinctive, holds at all sizes.
T4 · Sharp
Angular, no soft ends. Editorial / graphic design feel.
T5 · Slash
Score mark / tally. More abstract. Less "checkmark", more "scored off".
T3 Bold tick · lowercase vs title case · recommendation marked ✦
lowercase
Casual, friendly, handle-like. Fits social/community apps.
Title case ✦
Recommended. Reads as a proper brand name, not a handle. The capital S gives authority.
ALL CAPS
Energetic, loud. Works great for merch and banners. Too aggressive for UI header.
