1 Asymmetric Masonry Feeds

Break the grid.
Own the scroll.

Cards breathe at different heights — tall editorial images beside tight compact cards. The user's avatar and title block bleeds upward over the image bottom edge, creating a tactile 3D layer that makes the feed feel alive and editorial, not spreadsheet-flat.

4 Typographic Contrast

Giant meets ghost.
No middle ground.

Key metrics use Syne 800 at extreme sizes. Descriptive labels drop to Inter 300 with wide tracking. The contrast between them IS the hierarchy — no color crutch needed.

5 Micro-Interactive States

Every press is
an event.

Resting state: clean red outline. Active state: solid cherry red fill + multi-layer explosive shadow + scale(1.04) on the container. Cards scale up and cast a red-tinted halo when pressed.
