B · Double-tick standalone mark
✓✓ without letters
Two ticks alone — no S, no letters. Pure signal: found, scored. Think WhatsApp blue ticks but for collecting. Smallest usable mark in the system.

A single long tick mark arcs across the entire wordmark — as if the whole brand has been scored at once. Grand gesture. Works heroically at large sizes.
