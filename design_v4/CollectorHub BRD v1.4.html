2Executive Summary

Collecting is a large, fast-growing, deeply social hobby — yet collectors today are scattered across tools never built for them: Instagram for showcasing, WhatsApp and Facebook Groups for community and buy/sell/trade, eBay for transactions, Discord/Reddit for discussion. No single product combines identity, community, discovery, and trusted peer-to-peer trading tuned to a collector's interests.

CollectorHub closes that gap. A collector creates a profile, selects interests, and builds a collection portfolio. A personalised master feed surfaces new releases, community discussions, marketplace listings, and events. They join communities, message other collectors, confirm completed deals, and build reputation through a trust system — not just a popularity count. An engagement rewards layer (Collector XP) gamifies participation and drives daily return.

Phase 1 launches with five categories — Action Figures, Designer Toys & Blind Boxes, Model Kits & Lego, Diecast, and Trading Cards (TCG) — targeting 10,000 engaged users.

3Product Vision & Objectives

Vision: To be the home for collectors — the single place to showcase what you own, discover what's new in your niche, find your community, and trade with people you trust.

Mission (Phase 1): Build a focused, high-trust community across five collectible categories that proves collectors will return daily and transact peer-to-peer.

3.1 Strategic Objectives

#ObjectivePhase 1 success looks like
1Reach critical community mass10,000 registered users; self-sustaining daily-active base.
2Establish the daily return loopMultiple weekly sessions per active user via feed, marketplace, rewards, and notifications.
3Prove trusted peer tradingWorking vouch/reputation system; steady marketplace listing and DM volume.
4Shift to user-generated contentDeclining reliance on admin/dummy seeding as organic post share rises.
5Validate category-fitEvidence of which categories drive most engagement and trade.

3.2 Design Principles

  • Interest-first. A new user with zero followers gets a rich feed on day one.
  • Trust is a feature. Reputation, vouches, and visible seller history are first-class.
  • Seed, then step back. Admin and dummy accounts prime the pump; the product hands the microphone to real users as fast as quality allows.
  • Earn your engagement. XP, ranks, and season badges reward genuine participation and create competitive return loops.
  • Realistic to build. Phase 1 scoped to a shippable MVP; ambitious capabilities deferred to Phase 2.

4Market Context & Opportunity

The collectibles category has moved from niche hobby to mainstream cultural and economic phenomenon. The designer-toy/blind-box secondary market alone was valued at over US$1.8 billion in 2025. The India toys market was valued at roughly US$2.77 billion in 2025; the broader collectibles market is forecast to grow at ~10% CAGR through 2033. The TCG market (Pokémon, One Piece, MTG) is experiencing rapid growth in India driven by collector/investor demand for sealed product.

Collector behaviour is consistent: show off what you own, learn about new releases, find others in your niche, buy/sell/trade. Today they stitch this together across Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Groups, Discord, Reddit, eBay, and category marketplaces. That fragmentation is the opportunity.

XPEngagement Rewards System — v1.4 New

New in v1.4 — fully implemented in prototype

A Duolingo-league-style engagement loop built around Collector XP. XP accumulates from engagement actions, drives a permanent rank ladder, and feeds into weekly/monthly competitive leagues that award season badges. The system is live in the prototype across Rewards.jsx, data.jsx, and the profile RewardCard component.

13Trust & Safety

13.1 Phase 1 Foundations

  • Transaction-linked trust. Trust built from completed Deals — deal-backed vouches weighted higher than social vouches. Social vouches are allowed without a deal but weighted lower.
  • Independent vouching. Any collector can vouch for another based on 5 relationship types; no prior trade required. Request a vouch from known collectors.
  • Ownership verification. Tiered: Claimed / Shown / Verified. In-app camera only for verification shots. Possession ≠ genuineness — explicitly stated on verification UI.
  • Photo integrity. Perceptual-hash duplicate detection + verified-photo watermarking.
  • Reporting & blocking. Report with 6 reason types from any profile; Block hides profile/listings/messages. Reports reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Community moderation. Admin moderation queue — review reports, remove content, suspend/ban bad actors. Community approval posting mode for high-signal communities.
  • Transaction clarity. Phase 1 transactions complete off-platform. Safety tips for meet-ups and payments shown clearly.

13.2 Phase 2 Enhancements

  • Reputation scoring algorithm over Deal/rating graph; verified/tiered badges.
  • Genuineness verification (NFC, expert/grading partnerships).
  • Dispute resolution + escrow with in-app payments.
  • Fraud/scam detection, seller KYC for higher-value categories.
  • QR check-in for events (ticketing system).

17Glossary

TermDefinition
Master FeedThe personalised home stream combining interest- and behaviour-ranked posts, admin content, and joined-community posts. Three tabs: For You, Explore, Following.
VouchA peer trust endorsement. Can be given without a prior deal based on any of 5 relationship types. Deal-backed vouches carry higher weight. Anyone can request a vouch.
Collection PortfolioA user's items (owned / wishlist / pre-order) with photos, value, and pre-order timelines.
ItemA catalogue-level collectible record a user owns or wants. Carries the ownership-verification tier.
ListingA sale/trade offer attached to a collection Item and surfaced in the marketplace.
DealA confirmed transaction between two users; the object that unlocks mutual rating and transaction-backed vouch weighting.
Ownership tierAn Item's verification level: Claimed (self-asserted), Shown (in-app live photo), Verified (passed integrity/challenge checks). Listing requires Verified.
Post typeClassification of a post: Showcase, Discussion, Review, Poll, or ISO. Selling is an Item action, not a post type.
ISOIn Search Of — a post type where a collector declares what they are hunting, with optional budget, condition, and city. Appears in both the feed and the ISO Board in Marketplace.
Collector XPEngagement points earned through platform actions. Accumulates lifetime (never resets) to drive rank tier. Also feeds into weekly/monthly league standings that reset per period.
ArchetypeA label derived from a user's XP contribution mix: Showcaser (posts), Connector (social), Archivist (collection), Trader (market). Changes as the mix shifts.
Season badgeA permanent circular medallion earned by finishing in the top 10 of a weekly or monthly XP league. Gold / Silver / Bronze / Finalist tiers.
CanvasThe scrollable screen background token (--canvas #F8FAFC) — distinct from --paper/--card-surface (card surfaces).
RSVPAn event response: Going (committed) or Interested (considering). No tickets or QR in Phase 1.
Condition ladderThe standardised 6-rung condition scale: Sealed/MISB · Mint · Like new · Good · Fair · For parts. Fixed values; free-text notes allowed in addition.
Return loopThe set of recurring reasons (releases, listings, rewards, events, discussions, showcases, notifications) that bring users back daily.
Super-userA trusted/high-reputation user who may receive elevated privileges (e.g. event approval waiver).
