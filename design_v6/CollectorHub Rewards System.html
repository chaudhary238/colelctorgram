CollectorHub
Rewards & Ranking System

Version 2.0 June 2026 Internal — Team & Partner Brief

This document describes how CollectorHub motivates, rewards, and recognises its collector community. The system runs on three interlocking layers: a lifetime XP rank that reflects a user's total contribution, a contributor archetype that reflects how they contribute, and a badge system that captures both permanent achievements and competitive seasonal honours. A rolling leaderboard keeps the community engaged week to week and month to month.

Design principle Every mechanic should feel earned, not gameable. XP is weighted toward quality actions (verified items, reviews, vouches) over shallow engagement (likes). Badges are specific enough to be meaningful and sparse enough to matter.

01 / How Points Work

Users earn Collector XP by taking actions in the app. XP accumulates over a user's lifetime and directly powers their rank. Some actions are one-time; others are repeatable daily or per-action.

03 / Contributor Archetypes

Alongside a user's rank, they carry an archetype — a label that describes how they contribute rather than how much. It is derived automatically from the breakdown of where their XP comes from (posts, social, collection, market). The archetype shown on a profile is always the user's strongest area.

📸 Showcaser
Post-heavy contributor. Shares builds, unboxings, shelf photos. Drives visual content.
Earns most XP through showcases, reviews, and photo posts.
🤝 Connector
Social glue. Likes, comments, encourages. Makes the community feel warm.
Earns most XP through comments, likes, and community activity.
🗂️ Archivist
Catalogue-first collector. Verifies items, documents the shelf, adds to the record.
Earns most XP through item additions, verification, and collection logging.
💱 Trader
Market-active. Buys, sells, vouches. Builds trust through deal history.
Earns most XP through listings, completed deals, and vouches given.

Archetypes shift automatically as a user's contribution pattern changes. A new seller who previously just posted will flip from Showcaser to Trader as market XP grows. This is intentional — it reflects current behaviour, not historical identity.

08 / Frequently Asked Questions

Does a user lose their rank if they go inactive?

No. Rank is based on lifetime XP, which never decreases. A user who earned Expert status will always be Expert, even if they log in once a year. This is intentional — rank is a trust signal, not an activity signal. Activity is reflected separately in the leaderboard.

Can someone buy their way up the leaderboard?

Not directly. XP is earned through platform actions, not purchases. The highest-value actions (verified items, vouches, reviews) require real effort and community trust. Shallow engagement (likes) is capped per day to prevent farming.

What happens to a monthly activity badge when the month ends?

It is removed from their profile's active badge display. It does not become a permanent badge — that's only for seasonal league finishes. The user needs to re-earn it next month. This creates month-to-month competition without inflating permanent badge counts.

How do seasonal badges differ from monthly activity badges?

Seasonal badges (Gold / Silver / Bronze / Finalist) are earned by finishing in the top positions of a competitive league — they stack up permanently in a user's trophy case and tell the story of their competitive history. Monthly activity badges (Comment King, Share Master, etc.) are held positions — you either have the current title or you don't. Think: seasonal badge = medal you keep; monthly badge = title you defend.

Can the Early Contributor badge be revoked?

By design, no. It is a permanent badge. If a user is banned or removed from the platform, their account is deactivated but badge history is preserved in records. Do not assign it speculatively — treat it like a founding-team credit.
