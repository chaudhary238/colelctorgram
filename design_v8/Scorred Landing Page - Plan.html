Scorred — Coming Soon Landing Page

Plan for team discussion before build

1Hero

  • Full-screen intro: wordmark animates in, tagline types/fades in ("For the collectors, by the collectors")
  • Big "Coming Soon" badge or countdown-style framing (no fake date unless we have one)
  • Primary CTA: "Get early access" → scrolls to sign-up section
  • Background: subtle animated motif (floating collectible-shelf icons, or slow-panning app screenshot collage) — nothing gimmicky

2Feature highlights screenshots

  • 3–4 scroll-triggered sections, each: one real app screenshot (phone-framed) + short benefit copy
  • Candidates: Feed & marketplace, Collection/Database cataloguing, Community & events, Collector ranks/badges
  • Screenshots animate in on scroll (fade + slide), phone frame tilts slightly for depth

3Why it's different

  • 3-icon row: e.g. "Built for one hobby" / "Verified trades" / "Real collector ranks" — short, no fluff

4Early access sign-up

  • Simple form: name/handle + email (matches the badge-credit approach from the flier)
  • Copy: "Sign up for early access — first collectors in get a permanent Founding Contributor badge"
  • Submit → confirmation state (no backend yet — decide: mailto, Google Form embed, or store to a sheet later)

5Footer

  • Wordmark + one line + social/contact if applicable

6Open questions for the team

  • Do we have a real launch date, or keep it open-ended "coming soon"?
  • Sign-up destination — Google Form embed (fastest) vs. custom form we wire up later?
  • Which 3–4 features are the priority highlights?
  • Tone: playful/bold animation vs. restrained/premium motion?
