2Executive Summary

Collecting is a large, fast-growing, deeply social hobby — yet collectors today are scattered across tools never built for them: Instagram for showcasing, WhatsApp/Facebook Groups for community and buy/sell/trade, eBay for transactions, Discord/Reddit for discussion. No single product combines identity, community, discovery, and trusted peer-to-peer trading tuned to a collector's interests.

CollectorHub closes that gap. A collector creates a profile, selects interests, and builds a collection portfolio. A personalised master feed surfaces new releases, community discussions, marketplace listings, and events. They join communities, message other collectors, confirm completed deals, and build reputation through a layered trust and engagement system.

Phase 1 launches with five confirmed categories — Action Figures, Designer Toys & Blind Boxes, Model Kits & Lego, Diecast, and Trading Cards (TCG) — targeting 10,000 engaged users. The core bets: collectors return daily if the platform delivers (1) a feed tuned precisely to what they collect, and (2) a trusted place to trade with verifiable peers, with (3) an engagement rewards layer that makes participation feel meaningful.

3Product Vision & Objectives

Vision: The home for collectors — the single place to showcase what you own, discover what's new in your niche, find your community, and trade with people you trust.

Mission (Phase 1): Build a focused, high-trust community across five collectible categories that proves collectors return daily and transact peer-to-peer, before broadening categories and monetisation.

3.1 Strategic Objectives

3.2 Design Principles

  • Interest-first. A new user with zero followers gets a rich feed on day one.
  • Trust is a feature. Reputation, vouches, and visible seller history are first-class.
  • Earn your engagement. XP, ranks, and season badges reward genuine participation.
  • Seed, then step back. Admin and dummy accounts prime the pump; the product hands the microphone to real users as quickly as quality allows.
  • Realistic to build. Phase 1 scoped to a shippable MVP; ambitious capabilities deferred to Phase 2.

4Market Context & Opportunity

The collectibles category has moved from niche hobby to mainstream cultural and economic phenomenon. The designer-toy/blind-box secondary market was valued at over US$1.8 billion in 2025, propelled by Pop Mart's Labubu phenomenon. The India toys market was valued at ~US$2.77 billion in 2025; broader collectibles forecast at ~10% CAGR through 2033. Trading cards (Pokémon, One Piece TCG, MTG) have added a new wave of adult collectors in India with strong secondary-market demand.

Collector behaviour is consistent across categories: show off what you own, learn about new releases, find others who share your niche, buy/sell/trade — increasingly peer-to-peer. Today they stitch this together across Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Groups, Discord, Reddit, eBay, and category marketplaces. That fragmentation is the opportunity.

8.8 Communities

  • Privacy modes: Public (anyone joins instantly; posts + members visible to all) vs Private (request to join; posts/members hidden until approved; locked preview shows rules + request CTA).
  • Posting modes: Open (posts publish immediately) vs Approval (posts queued for admin review before publishing).
  • Guidelines gate: Members must accept community guidelines (via Accept button in About tab or in-post prompt) before their first post.
  • Tabs on detail: Posts · Members · About. Locked private shows only About (rules + admins).
  • Admin dashboard (Manage Community): Stat strip (members/requests/to-review) · Requests tab (approve/decline with trust signals visible) · Posts tab (approve/decline pending) · Members tab (promote/remove) · Settings tab (privacy, posting mode, rules, archive).

8.9 Events

Confirmed in v1.4 — no tickets in Phase 1

v1.2 mentioned QR-code event tickets. Phase 1 events use Facebook-style RSVP only: Going or Interested. No ticket generation, no QR codes, no payments. Multi-day event spans (start + end date/time) are supported. Going/Interested counts shown on all event cards. Ticketing deferred to Phase 2.

8.10 Direct Messaging

  • 1:1 DM. Inbox shows avatar, last message preview, listing context (SKU/title), timestamp, unread count badge.
  • Chat view: listing context header (tappable → listing detail) · message bubbles (sent = red, received = white/border) · timestamp under each bubble.
  • Attach sheet (+ button): Photo · Share listing · Make offer.
  • Make offer sheet: ₹ input pre-filled at 90% of listing price; Send offer publishes a formatted offer message.
  • Deal completion in footer: Mark as sold/traded → pending confirmation banner → Simulate confirm → "Deal completed" system message → Leave a vouch CTA.
  • Safety note shown in thread footer: "First time trading? Check trust signals & ask for an in-hand video."
  • ··· menu: Block (with confirm/unblock sheet) · Report (6 reason types, submit → "We'll review within 24 hrs").

8.11 Notifications

Notification centre accessed via bell icon (AppBar right). 5 category cards (each shows unread count badge): Likes (heart/red) · Follows (user/plum) · Replies (comment/teal) · Vouch (shield/forest) · Other (grid/gold). Tapping a card filters the activity feed below to that category. All-activity feed shows by default. Each notification deep-links to the relevant screen.

8.12 Global Search

Full-screen overlay. Scope filter chips: All · Items · Posts · People · Communities · Events. Empty-query state shows Trending Now (5 numbered trending terms with post counts). Results shown in collapsible groups per scope, max 4–5 per group. Selecting any result closes search and navigates directly.

9.2Onboarding Flow

5-phase sequence. Full-bleed screens inside the device frame. No bottom nav until main app entered.

Phase 1 — Splash

CollectorHub seal + wordmark + tagline. CTAs: Create account (primary dark) · I already have an account (secondary) · Explore as guest → (text link, skips auth).

Phase 2 — Auth

Email + password fields. Toggle between sign-up and log-in. Social auth: Google + Apple. Forgot password → email link (15-min expiry). Terms + Privacy Policy note on sign-up.

  • 6-digit code sent to email. 6 individual input boxes — auto-focus advances on entry, backspace retreats.

  • 30-second countdown before "Resend code" activates.
  • "Wrong address? Change email" returns to auth screen.

13Trust & Safety

13.1 Phase 1 Foundations

  • Transaction-linked trust. Deal-backed vouches carry higher weight than social vouches.
  • Independent vouching. Any collector can vouch for any other via 5 relationship types without a prior deal.
  • Ownership verification. Tiered (Claimed / Shown / Verified); in-app camera only for verification; possession ≠ genuineness — explicitly stated to users.
  • Photo integrity. Perceptual-hash duplicate detection + verified-photo watermarking.
  • Reporting & blocking. 6 report reason types from any profile or DM. Block hides profile/listings/messages. Reports reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Community moderation. Approval posting mode; admin join-request review; pending post review; member management.
  • Transaction clarity. Phase 1 transactions complete off-platform. Safety tip shown in every DM thread footer. "Ask for an in-hand video" guidance.

13.2 Phase 2 Enhancements

  • Reputation scoring algorithm over Deal/rating graph; tiered badge programme v2.
  • Genuineness verification (NFC, expert/grading partnerships).
  • Dispute resolution + escrow with in-app payments.
  • Ticketing with QR check-in for events.
  • Seller KYC for higher-value categories.
