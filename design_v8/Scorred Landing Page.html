01 — The Problem

The hobby outgrew
the spreadsheet.

Your shelf lives in four apps, three group chats and a spreadsheet you stopped updating.

And nobody can tell you what any of it is actually worth today.

Scattered inventory

Photos in one place, prices in another, box-and-condition notes nowhere.

Missed trades

No way to post what you’re hunting, or find the collector who already has it.

Blind pricing

No shared catalogue — every release, variant and price lives in someone else’s head.

No reputation
