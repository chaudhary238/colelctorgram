Icon alts
Current — for reference
Circle + tick
Too close to todo/task app category. Works semantically but not distinct enough as a standalone mark.
Alt 1 · Tick breaks out of ring
Escaping tick
Tick's long arm exits the ring — scored beyond the target. The overshoot gives it energy no todo app has. Unique silhouette.
Alt 2 · Whole mark tilted 12°
Tilted ring+tick
12° rotation gives the mark forward lean and energy. No productivity app tilts their icon. Feels dynamic — like a score being posted in real time.
Alt 3 · Bold tick only · no circle
Grade-mark tick
No circle at all — just the tick, two weights (short arm thin, long arm very thick). Reads like a teacher's grade mark or a price-slash. Aggressive, collector-coded. Very distinct silhouette at small sizes.
Alt 4 · Score slash
S + score slash
A ghost S with a bold red diagonal score line over it — literally "scored". Minimalist, fast, completely ownable. Reads instantly at small sizes. The S is implied, not shown.
Alt 5 · Tilted escaping tick — combination of 1 + 2
Tilted escaping tick ★ recommended
Tilted 10° + long arm breaks out of the ring. Most distinctive silhouette — impossible to confuse with a todo app at a glance. The tilt gives it momentum (like a score posting). The escape gives it attitude. Reads perfectly from 24px to 512px.
